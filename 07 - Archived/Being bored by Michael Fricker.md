# Being bored by Michael Fricker

just be bored, and you'll level up

In this video by Michael Fricker, Michael states that in the new found free time he has he started to get bored with what to do. He'd sit in his room, stare at the wall until something to do came up. 

Michael states that in a society where so much distraction is thrown at us from all directions at all times its important to isolate yourself and realise what is actually important. In being distracted and realising no actual work is being done Michael would start to bore himself out. 

His ways of doing this are quite simple but also quite effective. First he eliminates his primal distraction, his phone. He puts it away in a drawer on airplane mode, out of sight out of mind.

He then empties his brain by staring at something information less, say a blank wall. Staring at this wall and letting yourself get bored will automatically fill your brain with options as on what to do. especially if you plan out your tasks and goals before hand. In combination with straight discipline boring yourself can be a great booster to your productivity!

Michael also says that "being bored is better than indulging in instant gratification exercises" which is true. being bored will clear your mind into envisioning in what to do and how to progress. For example when feeling bored you can indulge in watching youtube but this mostly does nothing for you towards your end goal. its better to be bored for 30 minutes than to watch a video for 30 minutes. Being bored for an extended amount of time will result in showing you what to do.

'doing nothing is better than being busy doing nothing' - Lao Tzu
