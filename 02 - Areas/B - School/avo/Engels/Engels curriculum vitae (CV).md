# Engels curriculum vitae (CV)

In this exercise we learn how to write a CV (in the UK) / a resume (in the US) to practice and sharpen writing skills.

A CV is very important for scoring a job considering most job applications take a look at your CV first. Its a summary containing all your work experience and education. This lets the employer know your qualities. Even including skills and interests can show what you are capable of.

### Preperation task:
put the phrases in the corresponding groups

> Proficient Knowledge of analytics software
