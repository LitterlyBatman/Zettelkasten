changing hands

tags: [[literatuur]] [[]]

i heard somewhere that
your skin cells are
recycled
every seven years
and i stare down at my hands
that have gotten me through 
every year of my life
and realize that even
my skin isnt forever
because these
aren't the same hands that i was born with
soon enough
my fingers will have never 
touched the walls of my childhood
and i crumble into the thought 
that only physical evidence of me
will dissapear
every seven years
but life must go on, 
i can't stop for some
silly girl scared of her hands

-marie
