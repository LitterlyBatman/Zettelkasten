 Latin _solus_ “alone” and _loqui_ “to speak”

"An unexamined life is not worth living, so know thyself" - Augustine

Soliloquis is the act of 
"speakings ones thoughts aloud when by oneself or regardless of any hearers" 

the reflection on your own thoughts and beliefs. finding out why you believe what you believe and the reasons behind why you think what you think

used in theatre when a character speaks their thoughts absend of any other characters on stage thus "by oneself" 
e.g. : "to be or not to be, that, is the question" - Shake'spear

often portrayed in a dialog directed at oneself (or a monolog)
