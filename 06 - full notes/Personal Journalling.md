# Journalling

Journalling for many people is a act of self improvement and self help. Writing down your thoughts and reflecting on them later is a great way to improve on previous mistakes. Writing also helps clear your mind, improve hand eye cordination, writing skills, reading skills, attention span. One hell of a habbit.  

With writing looking like such a super power why are there still so many people not doing it. Starting a journal knowing that with each page you write you lose 1 it can be intimidating for new writers. 

New writers should note ;) that writing your journal is about you and your the only one to read it. and no one will judge your journal or give it a grade so be free in your writing.

"If every time someone asks you a question you try to say the correct answer, your whole life is a test. " 

"trying is failing, there are no ifs, no should's or could's / it just is, and we just are"

## 1 Write about something in your day

- something that happened to you during the venture of your day, maybe you grabbed a coffee in the city and read something interesting in the newspaper. something notable worth writing down.

## 2 write about something that went well

- maybe you got a good grade as a result of good learning, you we're able to apply a good habit or maybe you helped an elderly cross the street!

## 3 write about something that didn't go well

- there are things that might go less well, these things are important to adress. reflecting on what happened (and maybe on how it was fixed) gives allot of insight on how we handle problems and can handle them. This way we improve are problem solving skills and get to know ourselves a little better along the way
