# Enter flowstate

The flowstate is known as the mind state of ultimate engagement, creativity and concentration. people who find themselves in this state often have trouble stopping the task at hand. Entering a flow state has reported improved creativity, self esteem, feelings of accomplishment and acedemic performance. 

The flow state is even seen as a altered state of conciousness by various psychologists. During flow, time seems to fly by. self awareness loweress which connects to creativity and peak performance. Flow state also has neurological changes seen in the parts of the brain that are responsible for attention, self awereness and self conciousness. 

Researchers have been studying this phenomenon for decades and they still cant seem to find what really activates it. Research shows that you find flow quicker in tasks that you enjoy or find meaning in. It also corrolates to a skill/challenge scale, is a task too easy? you will get deminished, task too hard? you will get demotivated. Having a clear goal with checkpoints allong the way can easily help with finding a flow state. Quiet enviroments with no distractions and a clear path to follow your tasks can help with finding the flow.
