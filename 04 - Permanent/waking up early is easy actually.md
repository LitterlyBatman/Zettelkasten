# waking up early is easy actually

waking up early is one of lifes greatest medicine, better sleep literally means better health. You wake up early you have the time to do all the things you keep doing at the end of the day and you get some free time with it. 

## The problem
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

>Unoptimal Schedule![[Pasted image 20250121122256.png]]

Im mostly waking up, as late as i can get away with, like most people. Also, going to bed as late as i can get away with. This leaves you with a huge block of free time in which it is very easy to waste time. 

.1) it's the end of the day
.2) Other people are awake and can distract you
.3) you're tired from work / school
.4) all your free time is clumped in to one bit

Allot people, Including me, put off adapting to a more early-bird schedule trying to focus on other things like completing other goals treating waking up the same as those goals. A thing to be achieved. Although i believe waking up early is awesome actually and the tool to reach just those goals you putting before waking up early.

it will put you closer to reaching all those goals by:
- making you less sleep deprived
- giving you more energy
- helping recovery 
- assisting hypertrophy
- less time for social media
- improving hormone regulation
- giving you more time to pursue hobbies
- giving you time to work at peak focus levels


>Optimal Schedule![[Pasted image 20250121122313.png]]

Advice on achieving this schedule online suggests that you make and follow a routine. Take a warm shower, read a book, meditate. You end up doing this and are in bed 3 hours earlier than normal. You fall asleep a restless hour later, get up, go for that run and fast till noon. The "Optimal plan". you have just brought upon yourself a set of instantaneous changes in you lifestyle which you will have to keep repeating every day for the rest of you life. 

This big of a change usually ends up with people giving up and falling back into their old habbits. The trick to achieving a such a big change is training and slowly acclimatising yourself to waking up early. 

to help with this you can start by using a one alarm method. its fairly simple since as the name suggests. You only use one alarm.

you can practice this at any of time of day when you have some spare time on your hands and your alone. If you're already conditioned to ignoring your alarm and snoozing it i suggest starting over with a new sound.

start by designing a morning routine, some things you do after turning off your alarm. for example:
> .1) get out of bed
> .2) turn on the lights
> .3) wash your face / hair
> .4) stretching
> .5) practising gratitude
> 	- practising gratitude is a exercise i use in the morning to ground me to what I have and appreciate. I ask myself what I'm grateful for, and name three things and why.
> .6) affirmations
> 	- affirmations are a set =of quotes or sentences maybe words that remind you of who you should be, I've been trying to incorporate repeating these in my schedule
> .7) breathwork

training this "one alarm" thing is as easy as setting the alarm. Set one for a minute, go lay in bed, lights out everything. Then once your alarm goes practise the whole routine you've got for yourself and start over once you're done.
